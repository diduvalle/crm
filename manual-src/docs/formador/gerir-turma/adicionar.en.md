# Adding an individual trainee

To create access one at a time, without the import file.

## Step by step

1. **Settings → Class trainees → Add individually**.
2. Fill in:
    - **Name** *(required)* and **Surname**.
    - **Email (IEFP)** - the primary one (the automatic username and password derive from it).
    - **Personal email** *(optional)* - the **invite is sent to both** emails.
    - **Username** - if you leave it empty, it is **generated automatically** from the name (e.g.: `joao.silva`).
    - **Password** *(required)*.
    - **Role** - Trainee (default) or Trainer.
3. **Add to class**.

The trainee appears in the list, ready to log in with the link `?t=…`.

!!! note "Two emails, one login"
    **Login is always by username**, not by email - so a trainee can have both the IEFP and a personal email without any confusion. The IEFP one stays primary; **both receive the invite** when you send it.

!!! info "You send the invite, when you want to"
    Adding a trainee **creates the account but does not send** the email. In the class list, the **Invite** column shows *To send* or *Sent on…*. Send them **one by one** (the row's **Send invite** button) or **all at once** (the **Send N invite(s)** button at the top of the list).

!!! tip "Co-trainer"
    To have a **co-trainer**, choose the *Trainer* role - they will have management access to the class.
