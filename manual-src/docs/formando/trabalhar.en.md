# Working in the CRM

Your workspace has several **modules**, in the sidebar. Here's what each one does - for the full detail, see **[Features](../modulos/index.md)**.

## Main

- **Dashboard** - overview: KPIs, revenue, pipeline, shortcuts.
- **Companies** - entities with tax data (NIPC, CAE, address).
- **Contacts** - the people (customers), linked to companies.
- **Products** - catalogue (family → subfamily → item).
- **Proposals** - pipeline in **Kanban**; export each proposal as a **PDF A4**.
- **Calendar** - calendar and tasks.

## Marketing & Analysis

- **Campaigns** - marketing by segment (with a per-persona generator).
- **Email** - compose and track emails (opens/clicks).
- **Analytics** - funnel, conversion, CAC, LTV, *lead scoring*.

## Compliance

- **GDPR** - consents, data subject requests, RoPA, breaches. See **[GDPR & Pedagogy](../rgpd/index.md)**.

## Is my work safe?

What you do stays **in this browser**, on this computer. That disappears if you switch machines, open a private window, or if someone clears the browsing data - and **none of those is your mistake**.

So there is a **backup on the server**:

- it saves itself **one minute after you stop working**, and whenever you close the tab (which is when people usually lose things);
- it is **one** copy, replaced each time - no history is kept;
- it does **not** use up any of your submissions.

You see its state in **Settings → My work**, with the date of the last copy and two buttons: *Save a copy now* and *Restore from server*.

### If you open it and nothing is there

The app asks before doing anything at all:

> **We found your work**
> This browser has no work of yours, but there is a copy saved on the server from 25/08/2026 at 09:55. This happens when you change computer or browser, or when browsing data is cleared. **Restore?**

It only appears when the browser is **genuinely** empty. If you already have work here, nobody bothers you - and nothing is replaced unless you say so.

!!! tip "The third copy is the one that stays with you"
    The browser is one. The server is another. The **export to file** (same card) is the third, and the only one that depends neither on us nor on the internet. Worth doing before a big experiment.

!!! tip "Your profile is not sandbox"
    Your **photo** and **email signature** are tied to your account, on the server, not to the work you have here. Restoring a copy, importing a file or starting over **does not delete them** - they come back on their own at your next sign-in.

    That is the separation worth grasping: on one side the **work** (the sandbox, from this browser), on the other the **identity** (the account, from the server). They are different things and they fail in different ways.

!!! note "The trainer sees that the copy exists, not what is in it"
    The class list shows **who has a copy and from when**. The content only reaches the trainer when you **submit** your work.

## Tips

!!! tip "Experiment without fear"
    You're in **your** *sandbox*. Create, edit and delete freely - nothing affects your colleagues. To restore the sample data, there's a button in **Settings → Data & backup**.

!!! tip "Learning tooltips"
    Notice the **ⓘ** icons next to various titles - they explain the concept and point to the relevant UFCD slides.
