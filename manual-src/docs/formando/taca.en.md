# Your journey trophy

Bottom left, under your name, there is a **trophy**. It tells you how much of the CRM you have already tried - and what you have not.

![The trophy panel: the tiers as a staircase and the eight areas](/manual/assets/screens/taca.png)

*The trophy, the current tier and the bar to the next one.*

## These are not points

The trophy counts **areas**, not clicks. The CRM has eight, and demonstrating **one practice** in an area is enough to light it up:

| Area | What happens there |
|---|---|
| Accounts | companies and contacts, linked to each other |
| Catalogue | items, families, stock |
| Sales | proposals and the pipeline |
| Templates | the design of the proposal PDF |
| Marketing | campaigns, email templates, automations |
| Service | support cases and retention |
| GDPR | consents, requests, RoPA |
| Configuration | custom fields, entity, calendar |

Only **work in the CRM** counts - not attendance, not test scores.

## The tiers

| Tier | Areas |
|---|---|
| Starting point | none yet |
| First step | 1 |
| Picking up pace | 2 to 3 |
| Halfway | 4 to 5 |
| Almost there | 6 to 7 |
| Full journey | all 8 |

**The level never goes down.** A tier cannot be lost, and doing less leaves no mark of inferiority: the floor is the same for everyone.

!!! tip "Open the trophy"
    Click it. You get the tiers as a staircase, the eight areas sorted by the ones you have not visited yet, and the practices still missing in each - with a shortcut that takes you to the right place.

## What this has to do with the badge

When you submit your work, the tier travels **inside the submission**. That - not your browser - is where your badge's **stars** come from:

| Tier at submission | Stars on the badge |
|---|---|
| Starting point · First step | none - the badge is the recognition already |
| Picking up pace | ★ |
| Halfway | ★★ |
| Almost there | ★★★ |
| Full journey | ★★★★ |

The stars sit **above the emblem**, like on a football shirt. Only the ones you earned are drawn: there are no empty stars, because this is not a score out of 5.

## Cum laude

There is **one per class**, and it is not calculated: the trainer awards it to whoever stood out. That badge is **entirely gold**.

!!! info "The trophy is yours, not a competition"
    There are no rankings and no comparison with classmates. The trophy shows you your own path, so you know what you have not tried while there is still time to try it.
