# Win reason

We recorded why deals are **lost**. The opposite was missing - and the opposite is what you want to repeat.

## Recording the win

When you change the status to **Won**, the proposal asks for the **win reason**, with its own list:

| Reason | When |
|---|---|
| **Price** | we won on being cheaper |
| **Features** | the solution did what the others did not |
| **Relationship / trust** | they already knew us, or somebody vouched for us |
| **Delivery time** | we could meet the date the customer needed |
| **Referral** | they came through another customer |
| **Sole supplier** | there was no real alternative |
| **Response speed** | we were the first to reply |
| **Other** | *you must say which* |

There is also a free **detail** field - *"only solution with GDPR included"*.

!!! tip "Choosing Other forces an explanation"
    If you pick **Other**, the detail label changes to **"What was the reason? *"** and the app **refuses to save** while it is empty. A record that says only *Other* cannot be read, filtered or counted - it fills the form without saying anything. See the **["Other" rule](../empresas-contactos.md#other-always-asks-which)**.

## Where it comes back

- **Analytics → Win analysis** - the sibling card to *Loss analysis*, with the same percentage reading. Side by side, they answer the same question from both ends.
- **Account timeline** - the *Proposal won* entry now shows the reason next to the value.
- **[Pulso](../assistente.md)** - *"why do we win"* returns the top reasons.

In charts and in the timeline you see the **text you wrote**, not the word *Other*: a hand-written reason stands on its own instead of going into a common bucket.

!!! note "Why this is worth discussing with the class"
    Loss analysis teaches a team to **avoid mistakes**. Only win analysis teaches it to **repeat what worked** - and those are different things.

    Note one more thing: both lists share *Price* and *Features*. If price tops both, price is not what is wrong - **segmentation** is: you are talking to two kinds of customer at once.
